P2P WebRTC Signaling Architecture

1. Peer A sends "join-room(roomId)" via WebSocket
2. Signaling Server notifies Peer B ("user-connected")
3. Peer A creates SDP Offer & relays to Peer B
4. Peer B creates SDP Answer & returns to Peer A
5. Interactive ICE Candidates exchanged peer-to-peer
6. Direct SRTP Media Stream established (Audio & Video)
