Intelligent Buffer Optimization Techniques

Eliminate video interruptions with dynamic sizing, pre-buffering, and intelligent recovery thresholds

A. Adaptive Buffer Management

Continuously calculates the harmonic mean of recent chunk download times to estimate real throughput, preventing over-optimistic quality shifts.

B. Dynamic Buffer Sizing

Expands the target buffer capacity from 15s to 35s during periods of high latency jitter, while shrinking it during steady conditions to conserve device RAM.

C. Intelligent Pre-Buffering

Fetches initial low-bitrate chunks in an aggressive parallel burst to achieve sub-second startup latency (TTFP) before seamlessly scaling to 1080p.

D. Buffer Threshold Hysteresis

Maintains high-water and low-water marks. Prevents annoying rapid switching between resolutions caused by brief momentary network fluctuations.

E. Intelligent Playback Recovery

If a stall occurs, playback is only released once the buffer recovers a safe 3-second cushion, avoiding the dreaded stutter loop.
