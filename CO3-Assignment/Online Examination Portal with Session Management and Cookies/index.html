Student Examination Guidelines

Mandatory instructions to ensure academic integrity and smooth submission.
1 Session Security: You must maintain an active student session. Closing your browser or remaining inactive for over 5 minutes will invalidate your session on the server.
2 Timer Rules: The 10-minute timer starts immediately upon clicking "Start Exam". When the timer reaches 00:00, all selected answers will be automatically submitted by the system.
3 Navigation Palette: You can jump directly to any question by clicking its number in the side palette. Answered questions turn purple; unanswered questions remain light slate.
4 Submission Integrity: Do not refresh or use browser back/forward buttons during the examination. Multiple simultaneous attempts are automatically prevented by the servlet container.
