Admission Process (2026-27)

Four simple steps to secure your engineering seat

1

Online Application

Fill out the registration form with your personal details, academic scores (Class 10 & 12), and preferred branches.

2

Server Verification

Java Servlet validates all credentials, ensures unique register numbers, and computes merit rank eligibility.

3

Department Allotment

Seats are allocated based on Class 12 PCM cutoff marks and student preferences across engineering disciplines.

4

Confirmation Slip

Instant digital confirmation generated with official registration number, ready for record viewing and printing.

Eligibility Criteria

Academic prerequisites for admission

  • ✔ Class 10: Pass certificate with minimum 50% aggregate marks from a recognized board.
  • ✔ Class 12 (HSC): Minimum 55% aggregate marks with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry.
  • ✔ Age Requirement: Minimum 15 years completed as of December 31, 2026.
