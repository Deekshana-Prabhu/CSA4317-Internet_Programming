About Sweet Crumb

Crafting unforgettable bakery experiences through slow fermentation, heirloom heritage grains, and timeless European techniques.

Our Humble Beginnings

How It All Started: A Jar of Starter & A Family Kitchen

SWEET CRUMB was born in 2012 out of a modest kitchen filled with flour-dusted countertops, wooden proofing bowls, and the crackle of a home oven. Founded by head artisan baker Clara Moreau, the bakery began with a single Mason jar of sourdough starter gifted by her grandmother in Provence.

What started as weekly loaves baked for neighbors on Baker’s Square quickly gained a dedicated following. People craved bread with real depth of flavor—bread that takes 24 hours of patience and fermentation, without a single commercial chemical enhancer or shortcut.

By 2016, Sweet Crumb opened its doors as a full neighborhood brick-and-mortar artisan hearth. Today, our ovens still fire up before dawn every single morning, honoring that very same foundational tradition.

Artisan Philosophy

The Beauty of Unhurried Baking

In a world obsessed with instant gratification and high-speed factory bread, Sweet Crumb stands for the slow, sensory art of handcrafted baking. Each loaf undergoes a delicate 24-hour cold fermentation process that gently unlocks natural grain sweetness and promotes healthy digestibility.

Our pastry chefs hand-laminate croissant dough with pure golden churned butter, folding and resting each batch through eight precise turns to achieve dozens of paper-thin, crispy honeycomb layers.

Guiding Purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Every recipe we develop and every pastry we bake is guided by our heartfelt commitment to our community and culinary heritage.

🌱

Our Mission

To nourish and connect our community by baking honest, wholesome, and exceptional artisan baked goods. We are committed to using 100% certified organic grains, unbleached flours, and zero artificial preservatives, ensuring every bite brings pure joy, comfort, and vitality to your table.

🌟

Our Vision

To be the benchmark for sustainable, community-rooted artisan baking. We envision a bakery where traditional craftsmanship is celebrated, local farmers and millers are championed, and every guest feels the warm embrace of home the moment they step through our doors.

What We Stand For
