About The Fest

Where Vision Meets Technological Mastery

Join us on 26–28 December 2026 as students from across the country compete in 16 technical and non-technical arenas.

Pioneering the Next Era of Intelligence

TECHNOVATE 2026 is the flagship national-level symposium hosted by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Apex Institute of Technology. From 26 to 28 December 2026, our campus transforms into an innovation ecosystem.

Whether you are architecting deep learning pipelines in the Coding Challenge or expressing your creativity in the Dance Battle, Short Film Festival, or Photography Challenge, TECHNOVATE provides the ultimate arena to prove your skills and win cash prizes.

  • ₹2,50,000+ Total Cash Prize Pool

Engineering Arenas

Technical Competitions

Six flagship technical championships engineered to test your algorithmic speed, research innovation, and engineering mastery.

RESEARCH

Paper Presentation

Unveil research in Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, Quantum Computing, IoT, and Cyber Defense before a panel of academic scientists.

Team: 1 - 3 Members
Cash Prize: ₹15,000
Date & Venue: 26 Dec 2026 • Seminar Hall A
ALGORITHMS

Coding Challenge

High-intensity algorithmic programming sprint. Solve complex data structures, dynamic programming problems, and speed tests on the live leaderboard.

Team: Individual (Solo)
Cash Prize: ₹25,000
Date & Venue: 26 Dec 2026 • Computing Lab 3
FULL-STACK

Web Development

4-hour rapid prototyping hackathon. Build a responsive, accessible, and high-performance web application based on a real-time prompt.

Team: 1 - 2 Members
Cash Prize: ₹20,000
Date & Venue: 27 Dec 2026 • Software Lab 1
HARDWARE

Robotics Challenge

Showcase precision kinematics and sensor control. Includes the Autonomous Line Follower speed sprint and the heavyweight Robo-Sumo arena clash.

Team: 2 - 4 Members
Cash Prize: ₹30,000
Date & Venue: 27 Dec 2026 • Indoor Sports Complex
TRIVIA

Tech Quiz

Multi-tiered intellectual quiz covering computing milestones, silicon architecture, operating systems, tech giants, and buzzer rounds.

Team: 2 Members
Cash Prize: ₹12,000
Date & Venue: 26 Dec 2026 • Mini Auditorium
INNOVATION

Project Expo

Date & Venue: 28 Dec 2026 • Main Exhibition Ground
