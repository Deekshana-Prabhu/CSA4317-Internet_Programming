DISCIPLINE SHOWCASE

Explore Sports Categories

Eight competitive sporting arenas designed to foster athleticism, collegiate camaraderie, and peak physical excellence.

  • T20 Cricket

    Cricket Championship

    Fast-paced 20-over leather ball tournament played under floodlights on the international-grade university pitch.

    11 Players + 4 Subs Central Oval
  • Football

    Football League

    11-a-side championship featuring strategic passing, tactical grit, and high-intensity knockouts on natural turf.

    11 Players + 5 Subs North Stadium
  • Basketball

    Basketball Slam

    Full-court 5v5 collegiate league featuring 4 high-octane 10-minute quarters with shot clocks inside the hardwood arena.

    5 Players + 5 Subs Indoor Arena
  • Volleyball

    Volleyball Clash

    Best-of-5 sets rally tournament with powerful spikes, acrobatic digs, and impenetrable 3-man blocks.

    6 Players + 4 Subs East Court
  • Badminton

    Badminton Masters

    Fast-paced singles and doubles clashes on BWF-standard synthetic indoor mats with feather shuttlecocks.

    1-2 Players + Subs Badminton Complex
  • Table Tennis

    Table Tennis Open

    Lightning spin serves, counter-loops, and rapid-fire rallies contested across 8 international STIGA tables.

    1-2 Players + Subs Recreation Hall
  • Kabaddi

    Kabaddi Premier Cup

    Authentic contact sport played on rubberized synthetic mat with 30-second raid timers, bonus lines, and super tackles.

    7 Players + 5 Subs Gymnasium Mat
  • Athletics

    Track & Field Athletics

    100m, 200m, 400m sprint dashes, 4x100m relays, long jump, shot put, and high jump on our synthetic track.

    Individual / Relays Olympic Track
