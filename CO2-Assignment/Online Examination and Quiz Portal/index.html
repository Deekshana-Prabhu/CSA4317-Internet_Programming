General Rules & Evaluation Scheme:

  • 1
    Question Format: The examination comprises exactly 10 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), loaded dynamically from the examination repository.
  • 2
    Marking Scheme: Total maximum marks is 10 Marks (1 mark per correct answer). There is no negative marking for incorrect responses.
  • 3
    Mandatory Questions: All 10 questions are mandatory. The system will prompt you if any question remains unanswered prior to manual submission.
  • 4
    Duration & Timer: Total allotted time is exactly 10 minutes (600 seconds). The countdown timer runs automatically and is verified against the system timestamp to remain strictly accurate even if browser tabs are switched.
  • 5
    Critical Warning: A visual warning alert will trigger when 2 minutes remain on the clock.
  • 6
    Automatic Submission: When the countdown timer reaches 00:00, the examination will be submitted automatically, and all answered questions will be evaluated immediately.
